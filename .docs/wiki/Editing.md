# Editing

The editor is a title bar with ten menus (File, Edit, View, Insert, Format, Slide, Arrange, Review, Present, Help), a toolbar that follows the selection, the slides panel, the slide with speaker notes under it, the properties panel and a status bar.

- **Slides panel**: click, Shift-click and Mod-click to select, drag to reorder, sections with named heads, badges for review status, comments and hidden slides.
- **On the slide**: marquee and Shift-click selection, smart guides (Alt-drag skips them), Shift keeps proportions, the round handle rotates, arrows nudge by 1 point (Shift by 10). Double-click to type; Esc finishes.
- **Text**: font, size, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, colour, highlight, alignment, bullets, numbering, list levels, vertical alignment, autofit (shrink or grow) and columns.
- **Shapes**: the Office preset shapes in a gallery, lines and arrows, fill and outline colour from the theme.
- **Pictures**: insert, drop or paste; Fill, Fit or Stretch; square, round or circle masks; grayscale; alt text; replace. Animated GIFs play.
- **Tables and charts**: a table picker with six styles; a chart editor for nine kinds with a data grid that takes pasted spreadsheet cells.
- **Video and audio**: MP4, WebM and audio files play in the show.
- **Arrange**: order, align, distribute, group, ungroup, lock.
- **Properties panel**: Format, Slide (layouts, background, hidden, transition) and Design (template, theme colours and fonts, slide size); Review (status and comments) and Agent (the agent switch and activity).
- **Views**: Normal, Slide Sorter (drag cards, collapse sections) and Outline (type titles and bullets, Tab to change level).
- **Several decks**: open decks sit in tabs, and New Window puts one beside another. Copy slides (Mod+C on the slides panel) in one deck and paste them (Mod+V) into another tab or window; they take the matching layouts of the deck they land in and bring their pictures, fonts, components and named styles with them. Copy Slides To on a thumbnail's menu sends the selected slides to the end of another open tab without leaving the one you are in.
- **Find and replace** across every slide and the notes, with Match case and Whole words.
- **Review**: each slide is Draft, Review, Changes or Approved; comments attach to slides and can be resolved.
- **Versions**: decks save as you work; File > Save Version names a snapshot you can restore.

Every key is in the [manual's shortcut table](https://github.com/nodemode-ai/formdeck/blob/main/docs/MANUAL.md#keyboard-shortcuts) and in the app under Help > Keyboard Shortcuts (Mod+/). What is not built yet (master editing, animation authoring, table cell typing, crop handles) is listed in [PARITY.md](https://github.com/nodemode-ai/formdeck/blob/main/docs/PARITY.md).
