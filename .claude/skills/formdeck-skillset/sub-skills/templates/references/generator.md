## From Design to deck

`build_template(design, w, h)` creates a `Builder` (size, colours, fonts, id counter, foreground and background roles), builds the master (`_master`: content background, motif decoration, text styles), the 21 layouts (`build_layouts`), and returns a template deck: `theme` (name, colors, fonts), `masters`, `layouts`, `template` metadata (id, name, category, tags, source builtin, licence Apache-2.0, description, `style`), no slides.

## Palette and theme

`palette(dk1, lt1, dk2, lt2, accents, hlink)` returns the twelve scheme slots (accents repeat to six; `hlink` defaults to accent2, `folHlink` to accent4). Dark designs (`dark=True`) invert the content foreground and background roles. accent1 drives title marks, big numbers and the first chart series, so its contrast on `lt1` (or `dk1` for dark designs) decides most template-level lint warnings.

## Text styles

`_text_styles` writes the master's title, body and other level styles: sizes, `+mj` and `+mn` fonts, colours by role, bullets per level (`BULLETS`), margins and spacing; title caps, bold and scale come from the design.

## Backgrounds and hero covers

`_bg(builder, which)` returns the content background (with paper or grid textures for those motifs) or the hero background for title, section and closing: `gradient` (accent sweep), `accent` (solid accent), `dark` (dk1 or dk2), `deep` (dark with glows), `light`.

## Motifs

`_motif(builder, kind)` draws decoration shapes for the content master or hero layouts: minimal, band, split, blob, glow, grid, frame, diagonal, gradient, editorial, bold, rings, stripe, corner, wave (`_wave_path`), dots, shapes, paper. Decorations have no `ph`, sit under content, and must keep clear of the title and body regions or they collide with text in every slide.

## Layouts and slots

`build_layouts` declares each layout with `L(semantic, name, elements)`: title (kicker, title, subtitle on the hero background), section (number, title, subtitle), agenda (title, numbered body with an auto bullet list style), content, twoContent (body, body2), comparison (heading, body, heading2, body2), imageLeft and imageRight (picture placeholder with the design radius as a mask, title, body), imageFull (picture, title, subtitle over a scrim), gallery (title, three pictures), bigNumber (title, number, caption), stats (title, stat1 to stat3, label1 to label3, named "Statistics"), quote (quote, by), timeline, process and team (title and a body placeholder; the compiler draws the steps or people), chart (title, chart, caption), table (title, table), titleOnly, closing (hero), blank. Placeholder prompts (`prompt`) are the text the editor shows on empty placeholders.

## Style hints

`style_hints` returns `{bg, fg, heroFg, card, radius, numberColor, motif, dark}` into `template.style`; the compiler uses it for synthesized cards (soft, solid, line, none), rounded picture masks, number colours and muted text.

## Samples and art

`samples.SAMPLE` is a 17-slide spec that exercises most layouts; `slides_for(deck, only)` compiles it into a template, replacing `@art1` to `@art5` with abstract pictures generated by `art.abstract(colors, base, seed)` in the theme colours (cached in the media store). `GET /api/templates/{tid}?samples=true&only=title` serves previews; the window caches them per template.

## Sizing and caching

Templates are built at 960 by 540 and scaled (`scale_deck`) to other sizes; `_built(tid, w, h)` caches the JSON per size. Changing the generator requires restarting the engine to clear the cache.

## Imported templates

`open_file asTemplate`, `POST /api/import?asTemplate=true` and Save as template write `Templates/[id]/template.json` with `template.category` Imported and `source` imported. `catalog()` lists them after the built-ins; `template_deck` loads and scales them. Their semantic layouts come from `pptx/read_layouts.py` name and type rules, so a missing semantic falls back silently at build time.
