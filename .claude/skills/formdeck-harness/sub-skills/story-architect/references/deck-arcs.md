# Deck arcs

Ten deck types, each with its beats in order, a slide range for a typical slot, and the layouts that carry each beat. Counts include the title and closing slides. Layout names are formdeck's semantic layouts.

## Contents

- Pitch (investor)
- Board or investor update
- Sales deck
- Product launch
- QBR (quarterly business review)
- Training or workshop
- Conference talk
- Report readout
- All-hands
- Project kickoff
- Template matches by type

## Pitch (investor) · 10 to 14 slides

| Beat | Layout | Carries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title | title or imageFull | Company, one-line what-you-do as the claim |
| Problem | content or bigNumber | Who hurts, how often, what it costs |
| Solution | imageRight or imageFull | The product in one sentence and one picture |
| Why now | content or timeline | The shift that makes this possible now |
| Traction | stats or chart | Revenue, growth, customers; the strongest slide, move it earlier when strong |
| Market | stats or bigNumber | TAM, SAM, SOM or a bottom-up count |
| Business model | content or table | How you charge, unit economics |
| Competition | comparison or table | Where you win, honestly |
| Team | team | Founders and why them |
| Ask | bigNumber or content | Amount, use of funds, milestones it buys |
| Closing | closing | The one-sentence point and contact |

## Board or investor update · 8 to 12 slides

Title (period and headline claim) · Highlights (stats: the three numbers that matter) · Results vs plan (chart or table) · What shipped or happened (imageRight or content) · Lowlights and risks (content, honest) · Decisions made (twoContent: decided / owners) · Asks of the board (content or bigNumber) · Next quarter (timeline) · Closing.

## Sales deck · 8 to 12 slides

Title (the customer's outcome, not your name) · Their problem (content or bigNumber) · Cost of the status quo (stats) · Your approach (process) · Product proof (imageLeft and imageRight, gallery) · Customer result (quote and stats) · Comparison (comparison) · Pricing or packages (table) · Next steps (process or content) · Closing.

## Product launch · 10 to 15 slides

Title (imageFull hero) · The moment (bigNumber or quote) · What it is (imageRight) · Three key features (one slide each, imageLeft and imageRight alternating, or a gallery) · How it works (process) · Who it is for (content or team for personas) · Availability and pricing (table or stats) · Roadmap (timeline) · Closing with the call to action.

## QBR (quarterly business review) · 10 to 16 slides

Title · Agenda · Scorecard (stats, then table) · Trend (chart) · Wins (content with a picture) · Misses and why (content) · Customer voice (quote) · Pipeline or funnel (chart) · Plan for next quarter (timeline) · Risks and asks (twoContent) · Closing. Use section dividers when there are three or more parts.

## Training or workshop · 12 to 25 slides

Title · Learning goals (agenda) · Section per module (section, then content, process for procedures, imageLeft for screenshots, comparison for do and do not) · Check for understanding (titleOnly with the question, answer in notes) · Recap (content) · Resources (table or content) · Closing. Training decks can carry more slides; each still holds one idea.

## Conference talk · one slide per minute, 15 to 30

Title (talk title and speaker as kicker) · Hook (bigNumber, quote or imageFull) · The idea in one sentence (titleOnly) · Three acts, each opened by a section slide, carried by imageFull, bigNumber, chart and short content · Takeaways (content, three bullets) · Closing with handle or link. Talks favour fewer words and more full-bleed pictures; the notes carry the script.

## Report readout · 8 to 14 slides

Title · Executive summary (content: three findings as claims) · Method in one slide (content or process) · Finding per slide (chart, stats, bigNumber, quote) · Implications (content) · Recommendations (process or content) · Appendix tables (table, after the closing or marked as appendix). Charts come from the report's tables via get_source t1.

## All-hands · 10 to 16 slides

Title · Agenda · Company numbers (stats) · Wins by team (gallery or imageRight per team) · Customer story (quote with a picture) · New people (team) · What is next (timeline) · Q and A (titleOnly) · Closing. Warm tone, names and faces, few numbers per slide.

## Project kickoff · 8 to 12 slides

Title · Why this project (bigNumber or content) · Goals and non-goals (comparison) · Scope (content) · Team and roles (team) · Plan and milestones (timeline) · Working agreements (content) · Risks (table or content) · Decisions needed today (content) · Closing with the first step.
